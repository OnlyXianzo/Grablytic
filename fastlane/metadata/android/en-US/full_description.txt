TrueStream is a high-performance, privacy-first media downloader and audio extractor designed for Android and desktop platforms.

Powered by an embedded Python extraction core with yt-dlp, TrueStream enables full-fidelity media downloads from over 1,000 supported websites with zero telemetry, zero advertising SDKs, and zero tracking.

Key Features:
• High-Fidelity Video & Audio: Download up to 4K/8K video resolutions, 60fps streams, and high-bitrate Opus/AAC audio extraction.
• Dual JavaScript Extraction Runtimes: Bundles Deno 2.7.7 for maximum performance and Node.js 25.3.0 for broad compatibility with complex website extractors.
• Multi-Stream & Format Selection: Intelligently parse separate video and audio streams, previewing formats, codecs, and sizes before downloading.
• Playlist & Batch Support: Download complete playlists with selective item filtering, reverse ordering, and shuffle options.
• Fluid, Responsive UI: Modern dark-mode interface built with Flutter, responsive across phones, foldables, tablets, and desktops.
• Hardware-Optimized: Native Bionic C/C++ libraries (FFmpeg, Node, Deno) tailored per Android ABI (arm64-v8a, armeabi-v7a, x86_64).
• 100% Free and Open Source: Clean, verifiable codebase under the GNU GPL-3.0 license.
